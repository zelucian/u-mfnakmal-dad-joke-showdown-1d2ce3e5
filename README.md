One dad joke, built for the dinner table. The setup is pure dad logic — of course he brought a ladder, he takes everything literally — and the pun hides in plain sight until the final word, where "steaks" turns into "stakes" and the whole sentence clicks. No explaining, no wasted words: it was chosen from ten candidates, polished head-to-head against a runner-up, and tightened twice more so it reads out loud exactly the way a father of three would deliver it, pause at the dash and all.

> I brought a ladder to dinner — I heard the steaks were high.

Character count: 60 (of a maximum 200), on a single line.

## Run it
